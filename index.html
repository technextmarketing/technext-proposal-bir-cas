Questions decision makers ask us.

Is Odoo BIR-accredited?

No software is. The BIR registers each business's use of a system, not a software brand.

Who files the CAS application?

You do, at your RDO. We prepare the system and the samples with you and answer the RDO's questions.

What if the certificate is late?

Go-live waits for it. Your booklets stay in use until it arrives.

Can we start with head office only?

Yes. We still recommend all three sites at year-end: one cut-over, one set of opening balances.

Do we re-register when Odoo upgrades?

Only for a major change to the financial side. We plan upgrades so you can file first.
