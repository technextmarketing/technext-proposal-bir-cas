Questions decision makers ask us.

Is Odoo BIR-accredited?

No software is. The BIR registers each taxpayer's use of a system, not a software brand, and in its advisory of 14 September 2026 it warned against "BIR-accredited" claims. Your business registers Odoo as its CAS and, if the RDO accepts the application, receives an Acknowledgement Certificate.

Who files the CAS application?

You do, at your RDO, in person or by email. We set up the system, prepare the samples, the audit trail printout and the system description with you, and stay on call for the RDO's questions.

What happens if the certificate is late?

Go-live waits for it, and the plan says so openly: your booklets stay in use until the certificate arrives, and go-live moves to the first Monday after it does.

What do we pay Odoo?

Your Odoo subscription, per user, billed by Odoo directly. It is separate from this proposal. We help you choose the plan and the number of users before you subscribe.

Can we start with head office only?

Yes. Each branch registers its own use of the system, so a phased start is possible. We recommend all three sites at year-end, because one cut-over means one set of opening balances.

Do we have to re-register when Odoo upgrades?

A version change that affects the financial side counts as a major enhancement, which needs an updated registration before you use it. Minor changes need only a written notice. We plan upgrades so you can file first.

What if we need more after go-live?

Requests come out of your support plan hours. Larger work, such as payroll or a new branch, is quoted in writing first, like the optional items in section 10.
